# StitchDesk business workflow

StitchDesk is an offline desktop pilot for tailoring counters and boutiques.

## Setup and measurements

Create the shop and owner account, then add customers. Create named Measurement profiles linked to each customer, for example ?Aisha - formal suit - October?. Choose inches or centimetres and enter applicable measurements; additional fitting details belong in Notes. Profiles are reusable live references. When an existing order needs its original fitting preserved, duplicate the profile before changing measurements.

## Order to delivery

1. Create a tailoring order referencing the customer's measurement profile. Describe the garment, cloth received, design and finishing instructions.
2. Upload a design reference image if required. Enter quantity, price per garment, booking date, trial date and promised delivery date.
3. The order amount is quantity multiplied by price per garment. Record only the advance already received; later receipts use Collect payment.
4. Print the order/job card and move it through Booked, Cutting, Stitching, Trial and Ready as work progresses. Keep fitting notes before final delivery.
5. Delivered locks editing but does not prevent collection of an outstanding balance. Cancel only unpaid orders.

Expenses record shop spending separately from customer collections. Reports show order totals, collected receipts and outstanding amounts; they are not profit accounts.

## Scope and limits

The pilot supports measurements, a single design image, due dates, order stages, advances/balances, documents and backups. It does not provide pattern CAD, automated reminders, fabric inventory, payroll, customer signatures or refunds. Measurements are references, not automatic historical snapshots. Validate measurements and printed documents in a real fitting workflow before relying on the application.

Initial payments explicitly select cash, card or bank. This choice belongs to the receipt created with the record; later collections choose their own method and do not rewrite earlier payment entries.

Garment quantities are whole numbers. Measurement values remain decimal. Trial and delivery dates cannot precede booking; trial cannot be after the promised delivery date.
